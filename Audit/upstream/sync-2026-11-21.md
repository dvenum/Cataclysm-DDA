## Recommendation: merge upstream as a whole

Since 175 of 184 PRs are worth taking, merging upstream master is cheaper than picking PRs one by one. Afterwards the fork is level with upstream, and the next sync covers only newer PRs.

1. **Merge.** Fetch upstream at `46391a6` and merge it into a worktree branch, as a real merge, so upstream history is kept.
2. **Resolve the 27 files** as the group reports describe. The main work:
   - **Map split** (six PRs): re-route the fork's 40 `map.cpp` hunks into the six new `map_*.cpp` files. MAP-P02 (lazy caches) has to be moved as one unit, or the game crashes.
   - **Dialogue split** (#89593): 9 of the fork's 94 `npctalk.cpp` hunks move to `dialogue.cpp`.
   - **`item_location` in use actions** (#89473, after #89509): convert the fork's EOC use action and its USE-B fixes, plus 18 test call sites.
   - **Wounds:** five conflict hunks (keep each fork guard), the `get_pain_raw()` move, and the test API updates.
   - **Xedra Evolved spell learning:** take upstream's files and re-run the fork's EOC-B14 weight fix by script, replacing about 65 conflict hunks.
   - **Combat:** depends on the owner's decisions below.
3. **Leave out** the three SKIPs and whatever the owner declines.
4. **Fix the upstream bugs listed below,** with tests where they can be tested, and fix the fork issues found along the way.
5. **Verify:**
   - build, focused suites, one full suite, and every mod set loading
   - re-run the `npc_weapon_eval` benchmark if partial dodge is taken
   - an independent review
   - then a PR

**Effort.** The refactor report estimates 7 to 11 hours of work for the large refactors, with build and tests. The wounds, mods and engine adjustments are medium or smaller each. Overall, it is about the size of one of the larger earlier batches.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Combat exertion: #89469 and #89519.**
   - **What upstream does.** It adds an always-on `COMBAT_EXERCISE = 500` activity level. A fresh fighter burns roughly 500-600 kcal per minute of steady fighting until weariness caps it. Today it is about 17.
   - **The clash.** The grappling port made TLG's milder rebalance opt-in (`EXPLOSIVE_COMBAT_EXERTION`, off by default). Its decision 5 is that "with the option off, nothing changes". #89469 breaks that and `tests/grapple_world_test.cpp`.
   - **Choices:**
     - (a) skip both PRs and keep the fork's opt-in
     - (b) take upstream's always-on level
     - (c) offer it through the fork's option, for example a choice of normal, explosive or combat
2. **Partial dodge and sliding-scale blocks: #89470 with #89640.**
   - **What upstream does.** It moves from hit-or-miss to graded hits: damage scales with hit quality, characters can deflect hits to another limb, and blocks reduce the hit on a scale.
   - **Hidden balance change.** It also halves head, eyes and mouth HP (60 to 30), along with bionic and mutant heads.
   - **If taken, these need adjusting:**
     - keep HARDTOHIT (CMBT-B12) for monster special attacks
     - keep the CMBT-J01 get-hit hooks firing first
     - decide whether grabs and disarms can be blocked
     - stop held monsters from blocking
     - fix dodge counters firing on hits that land, and fix the stale block state in six hardcoded attacks
   - **Choices:**
     - (a) take both with those adjustments
     - (b) take both but keep the old head HP
     - (c) skip both. Later upstream combat PRs build on them, so skipping means drifting apart in combat code.
3. **Wounds hold back HP: #89557.**
   - **What upstream does.** A wound keeps the damage that caused it from healing until the wound heals or is treated. A mixed-damage hit makes one wound per damage type, and `u_add_wound` no longer worsens an existing wound by default.
   - **If taken, fix:**
     - medicine healing can drain HP when HP is above the cap
     - a possible null dereference
     - the clash with HLTH-B01's spread damage
   - **Dependencies.** #89573, #89549 and #89651 build on it; without it they must be ported by hand.
   - **Choices:** (a) take it with the fixes, or (b) stop the wound C++ chain before it.
4. **Wound limb-score formula: #89651.**
   - **The effect.** Above about 75% HP, small wounds such as bruises and scratches stop lowering limb scores.
   - **Choices:**
     - (a) take upstream's formula
     - (b) cap the HP factor at 1.0, so wound penalties always apply
5. **Antiseptic and peroxide descriptions: #89539** (minor).
   - **The issue.** The text now calls them weak disinfectants, but their stats are unchanged.
   - **Choices:** (a) take the text, or (b) also lower their stats to match.

## Fork issues found along the way

- **Windows test builds.** Seven fork-only test files use `near` or `far` as variable names. Upstream renamed these in its own tests (#89397, #89491), because `<windows.h>` turns them into empty macros in MSVC builds with the precompiled header. The release build doesn't compile tests, so it is unaffected.
- **Monster grabs** with their own `condition` replace the base condition, which drops the grappling port's NO_GRAB and incorporeal guards. About 14 such overrides are already in the fork, and #89278 and #89642 add two more. Moving the guard into `melee_actor::call` fixes all of them.
- **`.github/workflows/comment-commands.yml`** has no repository check, so the upstream users it lists could label, close or reopen the fork's issues by commenting. It is worth disabling in the fork.
- **Open findings these PRs close:**
  - TERR-B13 (#89627)
  - HLTH-B19 (#89651)
  - half of MONDT-B19 (#89611)
  - half of MAGIC-B09 (#89658)
  - the container half of the zone-fetch mismatch (#89385); ACT-B07's vehicle half stays open
